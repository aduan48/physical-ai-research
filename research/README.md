# Physical AI Opportunities Near Colby College

Where can robots, drones, computer vision, and smart sensors fix real bottlenecks in industries around Waterville, Maine? This project answers that one industry at a time, with sources for every claim.

**Status:** 3 of 9 industries done (Forestry, Healthcare, Manufacturing). See [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md).

## Top 5 opportunities (so far)
Ranked by Impact × Feasibility, then shortest time to pilot. Ratings are the author's judgment.

| Rank | ID | Opportunity | Impact | Feas. | Pilot | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FOR-2 | Drone + AI forest inventory and pest detection | 4 | 4 | 6 mo | UMaine + Colby already run this research; forest owners lack remote-sensing staff [S13][S15]. |
| 2 | HC-2 | AI video fall detection in nursing homes | 4 | 4 | 6 mo | Published study cut time on the floor after falls by ~30 min; Maine fall deaths are 1.7× the U.S. rate [S43][S45]. |
| 3 | MFG-2 | AI optical sorting on food packing lines | 3 | 5 | 6 mo | Already proven in Maine: 4× throughput with about half the labor [S60]. |
| 4 | HC-1 | Camera-free in-home fall detection and check-ins | 5 | 3 | 9 mo | 23,500 home-care hours/week go unstaffed in the oldest state; Kennebec and Somerset are hit hardest [S37][S38]. |
| 5 (tie) | FOR-3 | Vision log scaling and lumber grading for small mills | 3 | 4 | 6 mo | Mills must grow output with fewer workers; tools exist but small mills lack them [S1][S21]. |
| 5 (tie) | MFG-1 | Cobots for small manufacturers | 3 | 4 | 6 mo | Maine firms lag on automation; Maine MEP, UMaine and Roux already offer help [S57][S66]. |

Full list: [opportunities.csv](opportunities.csv) or the [dashboard](https://aduan48.github.io/physical-ai-research/research/site/).
