Task guidance station

Concept model. A camera above a workbench checks each step of a packing or assembly task, and a screen shows the worker what to do next. It is meant for workers with cognitive disabilities in supported employment, so a job coach does not have to stand over every step.

How one step works

  1. The screen shows one picture step and the matching bin lights up.
  2. The worker picks the part and places it.
  3. The camera checks that the right part is in the right place.
  4. If it is right, the light turns green and the next step appears.
  5. If it is wrong, the screen says so kindly and shows the right bin again.

What the camera adds

  • Picture-step apps already exist, but they cannot tell whether the step was done.
  • Checking each step catches a mistake before the kit is sealed.
  • The help button calls the coach only when the worker wants one.
  • Checking runs on the board, so no video has to leave the room.
