Kitchen food-waste logger

Concept model of the pilot unit. A prep bin sits on a scale, a camera looks down into it, and staff tap one button for why the food was thrown out.

View

How one event works

  1. Staff drop scraps in the bin.
  2. The scale sees the weight jump and the microcontroller logs the change.
  3. The camera takes one photo of the bin contents.
  4. Staff tap a reason button and the light turns green.
  5. Weight, time, photo and reason are saved on the board for the weekly report.

Reason buttons

Overproduction Trim and peelings Spoiled Expired Dropped or burnt Other
