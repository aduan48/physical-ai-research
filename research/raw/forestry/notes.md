Source URL: https://umaine.edu/epscor/?p=9056
Status: READ ("UMaine advances drone technology to aid forest management", 2026-03-12)
- NSF E-RISE Maine-FOREST project. Drones with onboard computer fly under the canopy; AI detects trees, builds 3D model, plans path.
- Goal: "measuring tree trunk width, the presence of different species and any diseases." Research "still in the preliminary stages."
- Canopy-level scans do not give trunk width, "important for the timber industry"; under-canopy data "traditionally been challenging and time-consuming to collect."
- Lead: UMaine assistant professor of engineering.
