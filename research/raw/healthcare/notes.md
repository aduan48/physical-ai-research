Source URL: https://themainemonitor.org/mcep-care-worker-report/
Status: READ (2024-06-18)
- MECEP report: 23,500 hours/week of approved home care for older adults not fulfilled. Need 2,300 more full-time care workers.
- Section 96 "partially staffed" share rose 47% → 62% (Nov 2022 → Dec 2023).
- Counties with greatest need relative to population: Somerset, Washington, Kennebec, Aroostook, Hancock.
- 64% of surveyed workers named fair pay as most important recruiting factor.
